## What landed in this cycle

**The Author's Foreword**, requested by CT on 2026-10-09 after Grow Tech Fest in Chico.
New first section, `src/lib/data/book/00-authors-foreword.md`, about 1,600 words, titled
"Author's Foreword: Why Tell You All This?" and placed ahead of the Introduction.

It is written in the Argument register of `VOICE-GUIDE.md`. The arc:

1. **Farscape as the image.** A living ship the crew lives inside, offered as where biotech
   and AI are likely heading.
2. **Three real examples** so it is not only television: DARPA's early-2025 call for
   biologically grown space structures, NASA Ames' mycelium habitat work, Ecovative's
   mycelium packaging.
3. **The doom-and-gloom step.** A grown vessel plus a local AI pilot is the ingredient list
   for the aircraft in the film *Stealth*; the UN Libya report on the Kargu-2 shows the
   direction is already here.
4. **The pivot.** CT's question ("So why would I tell you about all that ...?"), then the
   case that things can go well: agency, boredom as a privilege, a personal assistant built
   from first principles, the weight-lifting visualization, maintenance cost halved twice,
   humans as explorers, settling new frontiers, snacks with friends, and the lightsaber as
   a benchmark.

### Verbatim passages (CT dictated these word for word)

Kept exactly and in this order, checked mechanically against the file with asterisks and

## Fact-check notes

Sources were secondary pages read on 2026-10-09. For a book that runs a fact-check harness,
the primary documents should be pulled before v0.7.5 ships.

| Claim in the foreword | What the sources support | Wording choice |
|---|---|---|
| Crichton is a test-flying astronaut swallowed by a wormhole and ends up aboard Moya, a living Leviathan ship | Wikipedia's *Farscape* character list: astronaut with the show's IASA, test of the "Farscape One" module, wormhole, pulled aboard Moya, a biomechanoid "living ship" | CT recalled a military or Air Force pilot. The source gives no military rank, so the foreword says "astronaut" and does not claim a service branch. "No clear path back" is the foreword's own phrasing of the premise. |
| DARPA call for ideas on grown space structures | SingularityHub, 2025-03-10: late-February 2025 call for ideas (solicitation labelled DARPA-SN-25-51), biologically grown objects over 500 meters, examples include antennas, debris nets and space station parts, fungal filaments among suggested inspirations, April 2025 workshop | CT described a "massive living space station". The source describes structures and station parts, not a whole station, so the foreword says "pieces of space stations". |
| NASA Ames mycelium habitats, Lynn Rothschild | Planetary Society, 2025-02-13: NASA Ames, Rothschild as lead researcher, mycelium habitats for the Moon and Mars | Project name in the source is "Mycotecture Off Planet". |
| Ecovative mycelium packaging, IKEA 2020 | Wikipedia: Ecovative grows mycelium packaging that replaces polystyrene foam; IKEA committed to the technology in February 2020 | |
| UN Libya report, Kargu-2 | LiveScience on the March 2021 UN Panel of Experts report: drones may have "hunted down and remotely engaged" fighters, possibly March 2020; report does not unequivocally say anyone was killed; unclear who deployed it | Written as "may have", "possibly without a human directing the final attack", "stopped short of confirming". |
| Ukraine drones choosing their own targets | Wikipedia's *Lethal autonomous weapon*: unverified secondhand claims of fully autonomous attacks, conflicting with a May 2026 report that humans remain in control | Written only as "drone makers in Ukraine have claimed ... and those claims are hard to verify". |
| The aircraft in *Stealth* | Wikipedia: 2005 film, uncrewed AI jet called EDI | CT's "the plane from stealth" is read as this film. |

**Softened from dictation, flagged for CT:**

- "The greatest people of all time mastered something. And a lot of them did it because they
  were bored." became "plenty of the people we remember for mastering something got there
  because they were bored." Same idea, no superlative claim to defend.
- "maybe half globally" spending most of their time on survival is kept, hedged as "maybe
  half the planet", as an opinion. A figure from a named source would be stronger.
