
Author's Foreword

Hello, and thank you so much for taking the time to invest in your own future. This book is dense and will take time to get through and process, especially with the cognitive dissonance that comes from learning many of the things you'll encounter here. If you're astute and aware, you'll recognize that many of these changes are happening rapidly. There is a double exponential growth occurring: the exponential rate of technological advancement is itself growing exponentially. As far as we know, this has never happened on planet Earth before, and it's impossible to predict exactly what will unfold.

That's why this book is titled Surviving the Singularity rather than Understanding or Controlling the Singularity. Full understanding or control is not really possible. The goal of this book is to present information that may be new to you and prompt you to reconsider and recontextualize your future and your place in this world.

The reason I wrote this book is personal. As an independent tech contractor working at the executive level for some large companies, I witnessed and experienced things involving AI that completely evaporated my sense of normalcy. Things haven't slowed down since then. I want to introduce you to concepts that may seem like science fiction or unrelated at first, but as you progress through the book, you'll start to see what's possible, what's happening in the industrialized world outside of America, and how we can all adapt to these changes.

You'll find facts, figures, predictions, reports from major organizations, and descriptions of technologies that might seem far-fetched but are very real and happening today. This book is your guide to understanding these changes and preparing for what's to come.

I also want to address the changing nature of work. Today, having a job might be one of the most insecure positions because many fear being replaced by AI. The truth is, your role will likely be transformed by AI, just as roles were transformed by the internet. Here’s an analogy that has helped many people understand what's happening and what’s going to happen:

Imagine you lived on a horse range, and every day it was your job to check on the horses and make sure they had enough food. One day, you went out to the stable and discovered that the horses had not only ordered solar panels and installed them themselves to electrify the horse stable and light it up, but they had also invented flying cars just for horses. They had even gone over to the neighbor's stable and taught those horses how to make flying cars. Now, all the horses that were formerly just running around in the fields are flying around in flying horse cars, having created a flying horse car society all by themselves.

What do horses want? They just want hay. Don’t you think that if the horses could invent flying cars, they could probably come up with a way to get their own hay? The horses in your stable might have been trained to listen to you and do what you want them to do, but nobody owns a Mustang. Wild horses are just as capable of building flying horse cars as any other horse in this scenario.

So, what do you do in a scenario where the technological advancement of a completely separate entity, species, or being is outpacing that of humans? A lot of smart people are really just hoping and praying that someone will figure out how to align these things. Others are buying EMPs. There’s a wide range of reactions, and no one really knows what’s going to happen.

That’s why I wrote this book, Surviving the Singularity, to help you literally survive in a post-AGI world. If you don’t know what AGI means, don't worry – we’ll cover that and much more.

To give you a sense of the magnitude of the changes we're dealing with, think about the impact of the printing press. The printing press revolutionized how information was disseminated, making knowledge accessible on an unprecedented scale. The internet brought about a similar transformation, connecting people and information globally. Now, we are facing a new level of technological advancement with AI, one that is poised to revolutionize our world in ways comparable to both the printing press and the internet.
