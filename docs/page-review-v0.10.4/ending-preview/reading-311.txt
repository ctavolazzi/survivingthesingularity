THE FOUNDATIONS
If youʼve read this far, part of your brain is probably running its own
premortem, looking for the flaw that lets it off the hook. The neigh-
borhood factories canʼt bootstrap their supply chains. Somebody with
more guns takes the greenhouse. People lose their minds without a
nine-to-five. The powerful crush any alternative that gets big enough
to notice.
Youʼre right to worry about those things. Theyʼre real vulnerabilities.
But using them as a reason to do nothing is fear wearing the costume
of  caution.  Name  the  vulnerability  out  loud,  then  build  against  it
anyway. Thatʼs the whole difference between a premortem and an
excuse.
What could stall it
Iʼm  not  selling  a  certainty.  Hereʼs  what  could  slow  or  stop  the
machine side of this story, and itʼs worth knowing:
War. A big enough war stalls everything, this included.
The chip bottleneck. A huge share of the worldʼs most advanced
chips come out of a handful of fabs in Taiwan. A major disruption
could constrain supply; its effect would depend on the damage,
inventories, and alternative capacity.
