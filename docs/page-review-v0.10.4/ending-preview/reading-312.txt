The Adequate Level of Care
So hereʼs the standard I want to measure a community by. Iʼve been
calling it the  Adequate Level of Care: can this place make sure the
people who live here have food, shelter, and basic care, dependably,
including through a bad week when the long supply lines hiccup?
Stop  measuring  success  only  by  GDP.  GDP  can  rise  while  a  lot  of
people get more precarious. Ask instead whether your community
can provide an Adequate Level of Care to the people in it, and how
much of that provision depends on a job, a truck from far away, or
one generous person.
