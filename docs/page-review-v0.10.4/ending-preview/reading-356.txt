P-12: Bronze Age disruption
A. Bernard Knapp and Sturt W . Manning, Crisis in Context: The End of the Late
Bronze Age in the Eastern Mediterranean, American Journal of Archaeology 120

and the interaction of possible causes. The case is not evidence of a single
synchronized collapse caused by one variable.

P-13: Trithemius and media
Johannes Trithemius, De Laude Scriptorum, Mainz: Peter Friedberg, 1494, digi-

hostility to printing.
