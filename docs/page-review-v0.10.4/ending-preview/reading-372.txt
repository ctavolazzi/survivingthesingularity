The proposal
Use AI and robots to make survival more secure. Start with food: ade-
quate food available to everyone, regardless of employment or money. A
person who loses a job should still be able to eat. Restaurants, paid work,
and meals sold above the baseline can coexist with that.
Free to the person eating doesnʼt mean free to provide. Equipment, main-
tenance,  remaining  labor,  and  delivery  all  need  dependable  funding.
Cheaper production doesnʼt settle who gets access; people do.
Free to the person eating still leaves a working ledger: equipment, maintenance, remaining
labor, and delivery need dependable funding.
