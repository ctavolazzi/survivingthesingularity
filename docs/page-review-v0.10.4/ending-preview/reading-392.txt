APPENDIX F
Glossary
Every term this book leans on, in plain words, with the chapter that explains it.
Definitions here follow how this book uses a word; other writers may use it dif-
ferently.
Accelerating Change. One of three ways of thinking about the Singularity:
technology feeds on itself and speeds up, so you can read the curve and
name a date. This book declines it as a planning tool. (Chapter 1)
Adequate  Level  of  Care. The bookʼs yardstick for a community: can it
make sure the people who live there have food, shelter, and basic care,
dependably, including through a bad week? (Chapter 18)
AGI (artificial general intelligence). A system that can do the range of
intellectual work people can. The authorʼs forecast for it is U.S. Thanks-
giving, November 25, 2027; itʼs a forecast, not a finding. (Chapters 1 and 2)
Algorithmic  judo. Wrapping  useful,  practical  knowledge  in  a  human
story strong enough that a recommendation system carries it to people
for you. (Chapter 10)
ASI (artificial superintelligence). A mind far beyond ours in every field.
In this bookʼs map, Stages 6 and 7. (Chapter 3)
Cognitive firewall. Treating your attention the way a security engineer
treats a critical server: batch your inputs, prefer text, and control what
crosses into your head. (Chapter 8)
Community  land  trust. Usually  a  nonprofit,  governed  partly  by  the
people who live on and around its land. In a common housing model, it
holds the land while households own homes under long ground leases
with resale restrictions. Other uses need their own agreements. (Chapters
12 and 15)
Create Over Consume. The protocol of spending your attention and hours
making  things  (writing,  building,  recording,  teaching)  instead  of  only
absorbing whatʼs fed to you. (Chapter 10)
