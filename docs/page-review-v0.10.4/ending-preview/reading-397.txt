Your First Year
The twenty-four precedents contain seventy-two practices. This year plan selects
and adapts some of them, alongside a few chapter exercises: twelve months,
each with a theme and a handful of actions. It isnʼt a complete checklist of all
seventy-two. The tag after an action (P-12, say) points back to its story. Choose
one action to begin each month, then add others as time allows. Move a month
if your season or your life needs it, and choose a scale you can sustain.
A year of practice, at a scale you can sustain. Shift the months to fit your season and your life; the
actions and precedent references follow below.
