The ladder with the bottom rungs sawed off
For people under twenty-five, the old deal, sell your labor and buy
your survival, has failed on both ends at once.
On  the  buy  side,  housing  shows  how  much  harder  securing  a
foothold has become. In 1970, by the Census Bureauʼs numbers, the
median home was worth $17,000 and the median household took in
$8,734: a multiple of about two. Census, Historical Census of Housing

On the sell side, the entry-level cognitive work a twenty-two-year-old
