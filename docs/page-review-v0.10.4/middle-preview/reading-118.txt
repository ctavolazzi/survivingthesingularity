A workshop is a beginning
Elijah  can  drive  north  because  he  has  savings,  a  car,  and  skills  a
workshop  can  use.  Someone  caring  for  a  parent  in  a  fourth-floor
apartment may have none of those. A serious proposal has to reach
that apartment too.
Martaʼs  nineteen  households  can  teach  us  how  people  cooperate,
repair things, and notice each otherʼs needs. They canʼt stand in for
every city, every climate, or every person who doesnʼt know some-
body at the table. Their work gets more useful the more it connects
with growers, kitchens, unions, and institutions beyond the shed.
So  find  one  connection  this  week.  Ask  an  existing  food  provider
where a meal gets held up: money, transport, equipment, staffing, a
confusing application, nowhere to cook. The answer names a job for
the tools and a job for the people organizing them.
