“Curtis. You know who the Luddites were?” She didnʼt wait. “Because
youʼre using the word wrong in your head right now, I can hear it.
Stocking knitters. Croppers.  Skilled trades, the machine operators of

wages were.”
“They broke things,” Curtis said.
“They did. Some of them threatened people, too. And a fire in a depot
can  hurt  somebody  who  had  nothing  to  do  with  a  layoff.  Iʼm  not
defending that.” She moved the bowl away from the edge, where the
dog had reached an ambitious nose. “Iʼm saying you donʼt fence those
people out. You build the floor Parliament wouldnʼt. Thatʼs what this
table is.” She picked her fork back up. “Pass the beans.”
