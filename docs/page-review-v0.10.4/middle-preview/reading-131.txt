The backlash is already in the street
Stage 5 isnʼt a theory. In San Francisco, a group called Safe Street
Rebel ran a sustained campaign against driverless cars from Waymo
and Cruise. Their tactic was “coning”: setting an ordinary traffic cone
on the hood of a robotaxi, which the carʼs safety systems read as an
obstruction, stranding it in the street until a human came to clear it.
