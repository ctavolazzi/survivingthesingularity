defending got paid.
The egalitarian pivot
Hereʼs the move the battle-line framing hides: readiness has to reach
beyond your own household.
“The  Entitled  vs.  the  Ready”  reads,  at  first  pass,  like  a  sorting  of
winners from losers. Read it that way and youʼll prepare the wrong
thing. A lone household with a generator and a rifle isnʼt ready. Itʼs
just losing more slowly, with better lighting. Readiness that ends at
your property line has a shelf life measured in weeks, because every
system  you  actually  depend  on,  food,  repair,  medicine,  security,
sanity, is social before itʼs technical.
