THE FOUNDATIONS
What I mean by mindset
When I say our mindset is part of the problem, I donʼt mean a fright-
ened person needs a better attitude. Gary didnʼt need positive think-
ing while his truck sat broken. His fear had information in it. If losing
a paycheck puts a household in danger, the danger is real, and the
useful response is to reduce it.
I mean the assumptions we keep turning into rules. That a person has
to keep selling their labor to deserve dinner. That making useful work
easier is a disaster unless somebody invents more work to replace it.
That  security  for  everyone  is  an  indulgence,  while  insecurity  is  a
practical  necessity.  Those  assumptions  become  budgets,  contracts,
eligibility tests, and the questions a room lets itself ask.
