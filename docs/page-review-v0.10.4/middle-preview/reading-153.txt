Protocol 2: Semantic hygiene
Some  information  is  built  to  spread:  packages  of  outrage,  fear,  or
tribal division that replicate from person to person. Swallow one and
your brain can spend hours, sometimes days, on it: imaginary argu-
ments, a spike of stress, a sour mood you carry to dinner.
