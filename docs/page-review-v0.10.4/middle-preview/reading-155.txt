Protocol 3: Batch it
Real-time stream (fragile) Batch processing (steady)
Instant reaction Delayed reflection
Constant interruptions Deep blocks of focus
Digital fragmentation Written notes
Shallow comprehension Mastery of a craft
The twice-a-day rule: check email and messages at set times, say
10:00 and 4:00, with a thirty-minute window to deal with them.
The paper log: keep a notebook on the desk. When your brain
wants to look something up, write the question down instead. At
the next window, run the whole list, write the answers, and close
the machine. You run the queries; the queries donʼt run you.
Long-form reading: whole books and whole documents.
