The  test  comes  on  an  inconvenient  day.  Someone  misses  a  shift.
Someone criticizes the committee. Someone shows up after everyone
else has done the work and sat down. What happens to their meal? A
promise  of  universal  access  needs  an  answer  that  survives
annoyance, fatigue, and disagreement.
Mesh, not star
A community isnʼt a hierarchy; itʼs a topology. A lot of corporate and
state structures are stars: everything runs through one hub, and if the
hub fails, every node goes dark.
