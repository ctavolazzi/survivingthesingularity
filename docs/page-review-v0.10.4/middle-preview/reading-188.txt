THE FOUNDATIONS
If your days go to consuming content about things you canʼt control,
the  days  are  gone  and  nothingʼs  changed.  The  line  between  the
people  who  come  through  this  period  with  some  footing  and  the
people who get flattened by it is a habit: create more than you con-
sume.
Before anything else, the fine print that isnʼt fine print: nobody earns
dinner this way. Thereʼs no moral accounting in which a person who
makes more deserves to eat more. Reading, resting, watching, and
receiving care are parts of a life. A restaurant needs people who want
to sit down and eat; a musician hopes somebody will listen. We donʼt
need a new contempt for receiving in order to value making. Create
over consume is a way to take your time back, not a toll.
