The breach began with an ordinary convenience. Their system had
no reliable stop between that convenience and everyone elseʼs private
life.
Elijah wanted his dashboards from town. Uptime, coolant tempera-
ture, the channel numbers, all of it on his phone while he sat at the
library  uploading  Dennyʼs  videos  on  borrowed  bandwidth.  Fifteen

front of the co-opʼs services. While chasing a bug he commented out

meant to put it back. He deployed with the comment marks still in.
