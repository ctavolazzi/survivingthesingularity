THE FOUNDATIONS
Useful help within reach
King and Queen of Your Own Robotic Court is a playful name for a
serious change in who gets help. I want the person who needs assis-
tance to be able to get it without first becoming rich, technical, or
employable in this yearʼs fashionable field.
The first assignment is food. A farm machine, a kitchen, a delivery
service, some planning software, and the people who keep each of
them running might together provide it. The person eating doesnʼt
need to own every piece, any more than someone reading a library
book needs to own the printing press. What matters is a usable claim
on the result. Can you get adequate food when your income stops?
Can you ask for a form you can actually eat? Can you get an answer
when a delivery fails? Can you complain without losing your next
meal?
