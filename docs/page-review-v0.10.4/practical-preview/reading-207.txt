“But what about the jobs?” Dishwashers and washing machines make
the idea of handing repetitive work to a machine familiar. That com-
parison doesnʼt pay the bills of someone whose income just disap-
peared, and their concern is real. Workers belong in the decisions
about how these tools come in and how people are supported
through the change. Thatʼs what this whole book is arguing for.
“Why not send machines into the dangerous work?” Where a suit-
able machine can keep people out of harmʼs way, thatʼs a good reason
to look hard at it, with the people who know the job. The deployment
still has to show that it helps, including the work of maintaining and
recovering the machine.
