In this chapter:

 • Two steel boxes and a lot of skilled work can make a small, owned home. The
   double-wide hack is how you keep them from being a coffin.
 • A bad weld looks exactly like a good one from six feet away. The vise is how steel tells
   the truth. Know your lane on anything people will live inside.
 • Shelter comes after food in this book, not instead of it. A home is more than a
   building: itʼs terms you can live with, for as long as you need.
 • Heat you harvest beats heat you subscribe to. The oldest technology in this book has
   a flue now.
