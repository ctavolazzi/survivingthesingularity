SURVIVING THE SINGULARITY

Build for every age youʼll be
Design the inside for the person youʼll be at eighty, and for the
neighbor in a wheelchair who comes to dinner. Put the plumbing in
one wet room with a floor that slopes to a single drain and no shower
curb to trip over. Before the wall panels go up, put solid blocking
behind them where grab bars or a fold-down seat might go someday,
so they can be added without tearing into the wall. Run wiring and
water lines in open, accessible chases instead of burying them, so a
leak or a rodent doesnʼt mean demolition. A house that has to be
rebuilt the first time someone gets hurt wasnʼt finished.

What the sketch leaves out

A drawing and a materials subtotal donʼt make a home. Foundations,
connections, weatherproofing, fire safety, ventilation, and permits
all stand between a clever plan and a place people can safely live, and
none of it is in the photograph. The co-opʼs files are a record to learn
from, not a license to repeat its cuts on another building.
Useful automation could help with parts of design, fabrication,
inspection, or maintenance, and each part has to prove itself in use.
And if it saves labor, thereʼs another decision to make: does the saving
become cheaper homes, shorter shifts, or a bigger margin for some-
body? A house can be quick to build and expensive to live in. A neigh-
borhood can gain new buildings while the people already there lose
the ability to stay. Count whether people can live in the result, safely
and affordably, not only how fast the machine puts material in place.

A city builds the ordinary

Viennaʼs municipal housing program between the world wars took
aim at things a resident could name without an architectural vocabu-
lary: running water, a toilet, daylight, a place outside the room. The
cityʼs archival history connects that program to changes in its consti-
tutional position, revenue, and spending. The ability to build came
with the authority and resources to keep a public purpose in the
budget. City of Vienna, History of Red Vienna
