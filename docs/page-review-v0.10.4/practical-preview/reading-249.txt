THE FOUNDATIONS
Walk into an American supermarket in the early twenty-first century
and you were looking at a historical anomaly. Avocados from Mexico,
blueberries from Peru, garlic from China, all side by side under
bright lights, misted by little sprinklers, priced at a fraction of an
hourʼs minimum wage.
To most of us, that looked like peak civilization. To anyone who
thinks in systems, it also looked like a high-wire act.
