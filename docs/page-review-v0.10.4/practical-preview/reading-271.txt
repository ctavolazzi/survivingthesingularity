Know the groundʼs history

Priyaʼs string line matters as much as her shovel. Old farm yards, rail
lines, parking lots, and industrial sites can carry lead, fuel, solvents,
and worse, and a healthy-looking plant canʼt tell you the ground is
safe to eat from. Before you grow food on land with any history, find
out what that history was and get the soil tested. EPAʼs urban-agricul-
ture guidance is a good starting point for what to ask. EPA, Urban

Cleaning up damaged ground belongs in the larger ambition, and

land is a job for people equipped to handle it, not a neighborhood
experiment, and a garden bed isnʼt where you find out.
