In this chapter:

 • Code and media can work while you sleep. Theyʼre the closest thing to employees
   that never get tired, and nobodyʼs permission is needed to start.
 • The machine flooded the world with synthetic content and created a shortage it
   canʼt fill: proof that a real thing happened to real people.
 • Trust doesnʼt scale, and doesnʼt have to. Files scale. Ship the files; keep the trust
   local.
 • Consent, privacy, and corrections belong in the publishing process. Neither a
   platform nor a file format guarantees income or permanence.
