SURVIVING THE SINGULARITY


















THE FOUNDATIONS
At some point early this century, a lot of us fell for one of the best
magic tricks ever performed. We were handed free email, free social
media, and free video hosting, and in exchange we handed over our
attention, our behavioral data, and a fair bit of our autonomy. We
werenʼt the customers. We were the crop. Every photo, post, and
video helped build the systems now learning to do our jobs.

But a magic trick only works while the audience looks where the
magician points. Step behind the curtain and it falls apart.

Why the machine needs you
Chapter 10 walked through model collapse: in repeated training that
replaces real data with generated output, rare patterns can disappear
and errors can accumulate. That isnʼt the result of every use of syn-
thetic data. The flip side is this chapterʼs whole argument. In a world
filling up with synthetic content, something that actually happened,
recorded by the person it happened to, is scarce. Your stories, your
mistakes, your local experiments, the record of what failed on the
ridge and why: thatʼs the valuable stuff now. Chapter 10ʼs algorithmic
judo is how you get it read. This chapter is about keeping control of it
once it is.
