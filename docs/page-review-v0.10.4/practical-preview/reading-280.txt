People before the story
Ask permission before publishing someoneʼs image, circumstances,
address, or request for help. Consent to receive food isnʼt consent to
become content. Give people a route to participate without appearing
in the documentation.
The next section handles the files. Permission comes first: no
technical setting makes consent unnecessary.

Media autonomy: keep your own press
Platforms are a temporary distribution channel, not a home. If every-
thing youʼve made lives on servers owned by someone else, it can
vanish with a policy change or a closed account.
Clean your files. Photos from a phone usually carry metadata embed-
ded in the file, EXIF data, which can include the GPS coordinates
where the picture was taken, the date and time, and the device. Free
tools like exiftool or Metadata Cleaner can strip it. That handles one
kind of exposure. It doesnʼt remove a street sign in the background or
a caption that names someone, so look at what youʼre actually pub-
lishing.
