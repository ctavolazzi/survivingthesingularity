Creative immunity
Making things isnʼt only logistics. Itʼs how you keep your mind.
The feed is built to keep you reacting. When you write, draw, com-
pose, code, or build, you flip from receiver to transmitter, and your
attention comes back to you. A community that stops telling its own
stories eventually starts believing the ones told about it. Keep your
history, your humor, your failures, and your hope in your own words.

Who gets to speak

A service can collect excellent operating data and still tell a self-
congratulatory story. The organizer reports meals distributed; some-
one receiving them reports that pickup happens during their shift.
