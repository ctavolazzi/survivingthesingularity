In this chapter:

 • The tools that survive the transition are the ones you can understand, fix, and run
   with no cloud, no subscription, and no permission.
 • Networks fail at the node somebody flashed in a hurry. Discipline you skip in fair
   weather collects its debt in the storm.
 • Debugging in the dark is a solved problem: halve it. Power or brain, this side or that
   side, measure, cut again.
 • Give the machines a worthwhile assignment: food first, then cleanup, shelter, and
   care. The proof is already on peopleʼs workbenches.
