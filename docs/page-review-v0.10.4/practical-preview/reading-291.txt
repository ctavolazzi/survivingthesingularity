The question for any proposed machine is what useful work it can do

The proof isnʼt hypothetical
Everything in this chapter already exists, and you can watch people
build it this weekend. This is the part the doom crowd never accounts
for: while the comment sections argue about whether the machine
ends the world, a growing number of people are pointing the same
technology at their own food and posting the plans.

One of them, working under the handle SECTOR 07, wanted his home
aquaponics loop to stop depending on a store. The system grows fish
and vegetables together, but it still needed bought fish food, so the
loop was never closed. His fix was to grow the food too: microalgae,
cultivated the way labs do it, in a machine called a photobioreactor.
Commercial units are priced for research budgets. He printed parts
