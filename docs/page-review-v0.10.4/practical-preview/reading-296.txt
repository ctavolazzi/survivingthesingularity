Encryption isnʼt automatically privacy. Meshtasticʼs default shared
channel key is public. A private group needs a fresh key shared only
with its members, and a plan for lost devices and exposed keys. Even
then, a group message isnʼt proof of the senderʼs identity. Keep sensi-
tive  household  records  off  the  public  channel  and  confirm
consequential requests with the person. Meshtastic, encryption and

To put one up: use a weatherproof box, a proper solar charge board
and battery rated for outdoor use (buy it assembled if youʼre not
sure), and the best antenna you can mount high. Height and line of
sight matter more than anything else; a well-placed node can reach
many miles. Then check what it can actually carry before anybody
depends on it.

The muscle: power you understand
Many small loads use low-voltage DC internally: LED lights, phones,
laptops, radios, small pumps. A conventional off-grid system turns
the panelsʼ DC into household AC with an inverter, and a deviceʼs
power brick may turn it back into DC. Each conversion loses some
energy as heat; the amount depends on the equipment and its load.
