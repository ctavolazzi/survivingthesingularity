availability of an online text do not by themselves settle an external-use basis.

The prior audit identifies unresolved external-publication questions for modern
quotations, particularly Vinge, Good, and Mitchell. Newly restored epigraphs
also require the publisher's applicable-use assessment. See Appendix B for
attribution references and docs/publication/RIGHTS-AUDIT.md for the dated audit.

The original illustrated cover is preserved because the v0.9.0 editorial plan
and subsequent editions explicitly restored it. Its provenance was unresolved
in the earlier image audit. The editable local handoff includes it as a narrowly
recorded exception, with the exact checksum. Its image-audit disposition remains
unresolved; no license or permission is invented. The cover is approximately
228 pixels per inch at 6 x 9 inches and has not had a physical print proof.

Four unresolved interior images have been replaced in canonical source by
existing original line illustrations. Their old files remain in the repository
but are absent from the new EPUB, reading PDF, and print interior.
