- No user accounts or sign-in. [Server-hook ruling](https://github.com/ctavolazzi/survivingthesingularity/blob/396b2383d1fdf33cf943b7f0ee870f1d1e465a2b/src/hooks.server.js#L4) preserves purchase-email identity. A book access password does not turn that ruling into permission to restore accounts.
