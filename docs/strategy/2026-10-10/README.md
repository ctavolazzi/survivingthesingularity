## Weights and ratings

| Criterion | Weight | What a high score means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Reader and project value | 30% | Direct benefit to a useful book, understandable offer, or dependable reader experience. |
| Credibility and reliability | 25% | Reduces a demonstrated mismatch, unsupported claim, or failure risk. |
| Work unlocked | 20% | Enables several next steps or settles a prerequisite. |
| Decision learning | 15% | Replaces an important assumption with evidence. |
| Ease of completion | 10% | Higher means a smaller, more bounded task with fewer external dependencies. |

Rate each criterion from 1 to 5: 1 = little contribution, 3 = meaningful contribution, 5 = direct substantial contribution. For ease, 5 means a small bounded task and 1 means large or uncertain work. Intermediate scores are allowed. **Score = sum(weight × rating ÷ 5)** with weights normalized to 100. The possible range is 20 to 100.

Small: roughly one focused session; Medium: several sessions or an external response; Large: multi-day work, author effort or recurring operations. Estimates exclude waiting for readers or proofs.

Dependencies describe completion or publication requirements. Research, diff review and preparation may start earlier in parallel. Conditional dependencies are stated separately.
