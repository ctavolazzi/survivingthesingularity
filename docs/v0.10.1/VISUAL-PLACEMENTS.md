# Six conceptual vignettes

Canonical Markdown remains the source. These blocks are proposals for the owner of that source to insert once. All six are original code-native conceptual artwork, not data charts, construction drawings, legal templates, or photographs. Each source SVG has an independently prepared light variant with identical geometry. Registry: `src/lib/data/book/visuals.json`. Regenerate: `python3 docs/v0.10.1/vignettes.py --proof`.

## Chapter 6: access gate

`08-chapter6.md`, after the paragraph starting `The point isn't to make everyone dependent on one kindly operator.` under `Security is something you can rely on`, before `A workshop is a beginning`. This focuses the governance question through a visual gate, separate from the earlier four-stage diagram.

```markdown
![A productive machine stands before an open gate and a plate. The gate names terms, price, eligibility, and delivery as choices that affect who receives the result.](/book-images/v101-visual-access-gate.svg)

*A working machine still needs an arrangement that lets people reach its output. Conceptual illustration.*
```

## Chapter 8: attention ribbon

`10-chapter8.md`, after the `Anchoring work` bullet under `Protocol 3: Batch it`, before `Protocol 4: Grayscale`. This is a visual metaphor for attention, distinct from the earlier focus-recovery curve and routine diagrams.

```markdown
![A ribbon interrupted into scattered fragments sits above one continuous ribbon, contrasting repeated interruptions with attention given to a chosen task.](/book-images/v101-visual-attention-ribbon.svg)

*A day can feel cut into pieces. The ribbons illustrate interruptions, without measuring a cognitive budget or a fixed recovery time.*
```

## Chapter 11: operating stack

`13-chapter11.md`, after the paragraph beginning `You don't need enterprise money.`, before `The memory math` This moves from the software discussion to the physical requirements, ahead of the memory arithmetic.

```markdown
![A sheet representing downloadable model weights sits beside separate layers for software, compute, power, and maintenance. A license still sets the terms for using the weights.](/book-images/v101-visual-operating-stack.svg)

*The model file is one layer of a working service. Running a useful service also takes software, hardware, power, and people who maintain it. Conceptual illustration.*
```

## Chapter 2: local copies

`04-chapter2.md`, after the paragraph beginning `The ugliest version of Stage 2 is somebody reaching for the plug itself`, before `Stage 3: The Adults Step In`. A visual interruption in the link shows what a local copy can, and cannot, preserve.

```markdown
![A distant server and a local folder are separated by a broken network connection. A local copy still needs a compatible app, a checked version, and electrical power.](/book-images/v101-visual-local-copies.svg)

*A copy you can open can outlast a broken link. It does not replace electrical power or a tested plan for equipment that depends on it. Conceptual illustration.*
```

## Chapter 12: shared land

`14-chapter12.md`, after the paragraph beginning `That's one route, and it's not the only one or the entry fee.`, before the `By the numbers: the ground` aside. The field is supported by an agreement, showing relationships rather than implying that ownership alone creates access.

```markdown
![A cultivated field rests above an open agreement with separate spaces for an owner, users, and neighbors. Consent, terms, and decisions people can contest support the arrangement.](/book-images/v101-visual-shared-land.svg)

*Useful ground comes with people and obligations. Shared use needs consent, clear terms, and a way to challenge decisions. Conceptual illustration, not a legal template.*
```

## Chapter 18: continuity calendar

`20-chapter18.md`, after the paragraph beginning `They wrote *funded through June* in ink.`, before the next narrative paragraph. The calendar reflects the fictional scene's explicit funding boundary; it invents neither a year nor a schedule of deliveries.

```markdown
![One calendar sheet marks funding secured through June in solid ink. A second outlines a proposed continuation, with a reminder to name backup cover, pay for the work, and tell households what is secured.](/book-images/v101-visual-continuity-calendar.svg)

*Ink for what is funded; pencil for the continuation still being proposed. The distinction matters to the households expecting a delivery. Illustration of the fictional scene.*
```
