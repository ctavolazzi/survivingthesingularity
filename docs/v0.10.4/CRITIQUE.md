# Gentle critique and revision

Surviving the Singularity v0.10.4 continues the previous edition with a complete editorial reread and 18 small revisions. The book's voice, fictional scenes, argument, illustrations and citations are preserved. This pass concentrates on places where the reader has to work harder than the passage requires.

## What is working

The strongest writing gives the argument a physical consequence. Elijah's demonstration, the failed weld, the sensor that needs correcting and Rosa's testimony make responsibility concrete. Those scenes have their own pace. They benefit from being allowed to stand without an extra explanation of what the reader should learn.

The ending also earns its hope by leaving work unfinished. A useful system still needs maintenance, a budget and people who show up. The distinction between an initial contribution and a dependable service gives the practical chapters credibility. The narrative scenes and that distinction remain intact.

The three reading routes help a reader enter a long book. The illustrated edition offers additional places to pause and understand a relationship. More artwork was not needed for the issues found in this pass.

## Give dense passages a little more room

The Introduction moves quickly between access to money, surveillance and eviction. Each connection belongs to the argument, but one long paragraph carried too much at once. A paragraph break now separates the access examples from the enforcement evidence without changing a word of either.

Chapter 1's phrase about the price of a trained person no longer clearing was less direct than the surrounding prose. It now says that the old price of trained work no longer holds. Chapter 5 moves into its library analogy through the idea of funding a service without charging for each use. Both changes make an existing point easier to follow.

## Keep repetition when it adds something

The recurring food and service argument gives the book continuity. It does not need a broad repetition sweep. Chapter 9, however, repeated a nearby checklist of what a dependable food baseline requires. That paragraph now advances to planning beyond the first five households and including the people receiving food in the next meeting. The preceding requirements remain in place.

Chapter 7 now states the cooperative turn directly instead of announcing that the next move is important. Chapter 14 gives a clearer pointer to its two closing precedents. These are small cuts to explanation, with the distinctive images and closing lines preserved.

## Make the exercises fit more readers

Some useful instructions assumed a reader's situation. Chapter 19 asked workers about their employer's unsold food and parents about their district's universal meals. Those actions now begin with a nearby food business and the meals the local district actually provides. The intended inquiry stays the same, with a workable starting point for more readers.

Chapter 6's group audit now asks for one observable change, who carries its cost and whom to ask when the answer is missing. Chapter 5 explicitly keeps unlike quantities in their own units. Appendix G invites the reader to start with one action each month before adding more. These revisions turn general intent into a smaller first move.

## Repair small breaks in continuity

Chapter 10 no longer implies that useful relationships cannot begin online while its own story shows one doing so. Chapter 16's closing practice refers to reaching readers who asked to hear from you, carrying the chapter's consent principle through to its final instruction.

Appendix D's obsolete Gary and Sal callback is gone. Its replacement asks where a historical comparison fits and where it does not. Appendix E's heading now covers Tier 0 through Tier 3. Chapter 3's optional reading route has its own paragraph, where it can be found without searching the briefing bullets.

## What remains a reader judgment

This is still a substantial book combining argument, fiction and a field manual. That breadth is intentional, and this pass does not flatten the three registers or change the positioning. The most useful next author review is whether the routes and exercises make that breadth feel manageable to a reader arriving with limited time.
