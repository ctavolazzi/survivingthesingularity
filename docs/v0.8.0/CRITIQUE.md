# v0.8.0 self-critique, first pass

Baseline: editable v0.7.5.1 at eb825cc, compared with September v0.7.4/r002 review package. First-pass manuscript: 49,430 words including the retained bibliography, 30 source sections. The reviewed PDF's 31 sections counted its cover/contents separately.

## What the first pass gets right

The argument now distinguishes adequate food independent of income from abolition of restaurants or chosen work. It acknowledges possible labor-market changes instead of promising every business remains unaffected. It distinguishes free access from costless provision. Five P0 families are resolved in the practical exposition by replacing unsupported prescriptions, rather than merely adding disclaimers. The personal forecast, speculative stages, fictional narrative and illustrative food trial have different jobs.

## Defects found by reading the result

1. The edit is substantially shorter, and several practical passages repeat the same meeting-and-review advice. The examples need an actual reusable task record and a concrete path from a modest trial toward a dependable baseline. Add these without turning the book into a universal institutional blueprint.
2. The first pass introduced a new subtitle while the source's other metadata retained the old one. Retain the established subtitle; identify the food-first revision on the edition page.
3. The preserved narrative still promises a greenhouse-controller build and radio troubleshooting flowchart that the new Foundations no longer contains. Repair those promises in the scenes.
4. Chapter 5 still says the earlier tower test proved heat was the only limit. Chapter 1 now correctly refuses that inference. Repair the callback.
5. Chapter 16 still publishes a universal municipal legal toolkit and the old treatment protocol; its invented license conflicts with the revised explanation. Rewrite the fictional handoff around bounded project records and actual permissions.
6. The land-trust scene promises forever in the county's handwriting. Narrow the fictional lawyer's claim while retaining Irene's agency and the honest repair that earns her trust.
7. The soil scene's new comparison still refers to a control bed with mulch, and the interpretation jumps from sampling to causal knowledge. Make the comparison about irrigation-monitoring differences, explicitly unresolved as a causal trial.
8. The monthly dinner count and total story chronology conflict. Establish a relative chronology and align the callbacks; retain the mother's Sunday call and Thursday journey with a four-day interval.
9. Chapter 17 conflates voice radio and LoRa messaging, gives a questionable firmware diagnosis, and rewards a solo hazardous repair. Keep the fault and the soup, but let a backup communication route work and postpone repair until conditions permit a paired visit.
10. The retained historical material still has sweeping claims, notably the Bronze Age case and some audience/psychology assertions. Narrow or replace them. Keep historical mechanisms as questions, not universal laws.
11. Appendix B is a large inherited research record, including claims removed from the edition. Move the full record to the editorial archive and provide a usable edition bibliography with scope labels and pointers to the historical source blocks. Do not call every historical quotation freshly verified.
12. A green source harness cannot observe clipped bibliography lines, an obsolete statement embedded in a picture, or a missing paragraph in a PDF. Inspect rendered pages and extracted text before delivery. Exercise negative controls for structural checks and page-boundary detection.

## Scope limits

This is an author-review edition, not a completed legal, engineering, clinical, or quotation-rights certification. All historical episodes remain represented, but condensed cases trade detail for defensible scope. The new edition has not yet been read by its author or an independent human editor. No publication, merge, or commit is part of this task.


## Revision outcome

The second pass addressed all twelve findings above. The task record in Chapter 9 makes the food shortfall explicit. Chapter 18 now follows a progression from a useful task to a service, broader provision, and a continuing commitment. These additions restore positive ambition without claiming that a small garden trial establishes universal access.

The established subtitle is restored. Story callbacks no longer promise deleted build instructions. The heat experiment remains limited to what it tested. The shared library carries bounded records rather than a universal legal toolkit. The land scene no longer promises legal permanence. Soil measurements are distinguished from causal experiments and numerical calibration. The storm repair waits for conditions and a second person, with a separate communication backup.

The chronology now spans approximately three years from the toast to the return to the bar. A final scene review also removed the implied fixed training-hours guarantee for structural welding and replaced improvised stove work with qualified assessment and installation. The soil scene's implausibly specific cheap sensor fix was removed.

Historical rules are narrower; the earlier bibliography is archived. The shorter book deliberately sacrifices some historical detail and technical exposition. That is the remaining major editorial tradeoff for the author's judgment, not a hidden implementation gap. This edition should be read as a proposed developmental edit, not as evidence that every deleted passage was worthless.
