## Editorial judgment

The strongest material is the narrative. Physical objects carry thought: the napkin, overheated tower, grandmother's stove, broken weld coupon, repair card, hardware-store bell, shovel, and broom. Preserve these objects and the people around them. They make abstract claims about automation and economic insecurity understandable.

The principal v0.8.0 weakness was a split between rich scenes and defensive explanatory prose. The scenes often retained sweeping guarantees while the bodies explained why those guarantees could not be made. Some bodies also printed the earlier editor's correction notes as though the reader needed to know which recipe had been removed. The remedy is affirmative explanation, not a longer disclaimer.

The author's new direction provides the organizing question: what would it look like for this technology to go right? Food independent of income remains the first concrete promise. Housing, restoration, and care belong in the ambition now, without asking people in urgent need to wait for food provision to be globally complete.

The most defensible economic distinction is that fear of lost income exposes the insecurity of making necessities depend on market earnings. It does not make every criticism of AI an economic complaint or prove that a tool cannot introduce a distinct harm. Keeping those categories clear strengthens the case for using the technology to benefit people.

## Self-critique after drafting

The first new pass still had several defects. Reading it back exposed a next-morning graph described as having risen for several days; an old callback to Gary's removed moralized diagnosis; a public-records inference that the data could not establish; and safety language that made an electrical-installation scene sound like an approval form. These were revised before handoff.

The revision intentionally gives Marta a mistake she can name. Her authority becomes more credible when she can revise an explanation rather than always providing the final correct answer. Irene and Frank now negotiate, rather than simply accepting rescue. Their agency is part of the argument about security.

The practical bodies still contain some repeated reminders about access and remaining human work. The final whole-book pass should decide where repetition is a useful refrain and where it repeats an argument already completed. The eight assigned chapters now have more distinct functions, but no isolated chapter edit can settle the book's total pacing.

## Checks performed

Source readback covered all eight assigned chapters. A focused check compared each against the preserved v0.8.0 baseline: original chapter titles remained, the same precedent IDs remained, referenced images existed, and no em dashes were introduced. A residual-phrase detector was first shown to catch a deliberately inserted obsolete phrase in memory, then passed on the final source. The checks observe those specific defects; they do not measure literary quality or factual truth.

The eight chapters increased from 14,509 to 16,130 whitespace-delimited words at this handoff. The additional space develops institutional choices, worker experience, recipient agency, and the wider humane ambition, rather than restoring unsupported technical recipes.
