## Chapter 15: organizing a harvest and asking who is free

The addition makes wartime gardening concrete through transportation, permission to use land, water, tools, instruction, summer maintenance, and preservation. A second paragraph of historical context puts gardens inside Japanese American incarceration camps beside the national campaign. This prevents a useful example of public coordination from becoming a myth of universal freedom or equal membership.

## Chapter 18: a fallback that actually carried the data

The addition follows the work from a dependency inventory to a specific transfer failure and recovery, then to a later rule for independent validation. It gives the chapter a documented successful response that does not depend on asserting a global catastrophe would otherwise have occurred.

The dated, bounded software problem differs from open-ended AI development. The text makes that boundary explicit and rejects the use of Y2K as an excuse to redefine a missed AGI forecast. This is a connection about operational knowledge and recovery, not evidence for Thanksgiving 2027.

Self-critique: a generic catalog of testing advice would repeat the existing chapter. The Oak Ridge case instead supplies a failure, its precise mismatch, a previously tested alternative, and a limited outcome. A scanned 1998 testing guide was located but not relied on for uninspected substantive claims. The sources above support the facts actually added.
