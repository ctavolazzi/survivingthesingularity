# Elijah's map, 2026-11-19

What he wants, how he goes after it, what stops him, what he does about it, and whether he gets
it. This is the text behind the drawing. Your rulings of 19 November are counted in: the
stroke, the sale, "Barry," the followers turning on him, and the fire.

**He wants:** To be believed.

**He is afraid of:** Being the only one who sees it. Being to blame for it.

**Where he ends, Ch 37 to 39:** He goes home. He stops correcting his mother. David tells him
the three were fine, and who Barry was: "You're not that powerful, Mad." He does not get what
he wanted. He gets what he needed. He no longer has to be believed, and he no longer has to be
driving. He asks, and he takes the answer.
