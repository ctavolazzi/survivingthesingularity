# Elijah's map, 2026-11-19

What he wants, how he goes after it, what stops him, what he does about it, and whether he gets
it. This is the text behind the drawing. The stroke and the sale of the farm are counted in.
The big map of everyone comes next.

**He wants:** To be believed.

**He is afraid of:** Being the only one who sees it. Being to blame for it.

**Where he ends, Ch 37 to 39:** He goes home. David tells him the three were fine: "You're not
that powerful, Mad." He does not get what he wanted. He gets what he needed. He no longer has to
be believed. He asks, and he takes the answer.
