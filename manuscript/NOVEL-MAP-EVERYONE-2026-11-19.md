# The big map of everyone, 2026-11-19

Every character on one map: what they want, how they go after it, what stops them, what they do
about it, where it leaves them, and whether they get it. This is the text behind the drawing.

How to read it. Elijah runs down the left side, one box for each thing he tries. Everyone else
hangs off the point where they come into his story. "To Elijah" says what they are to him.
The last word in each heading is whether they get what they want: Yes, Half, No, Open, or None
where the book never has them want anything for themselves.

Your rulings of 19 November are counted in. Chapter numbers are from the draft as it stands.
Anything here that the book does not say outright is marked in the files it came from: the
profiles, the arcs and the Drive findings.

**What the map shows when you read down the outcomes:** the four who try to buy it, own it,
steer it or force it do not get it. Ruby buys. Bidwell owns. Elijah, in Act Three, steers. Grace
ends by wanting it done for people. That is your theme, and it is already in the cast.
