## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

**Portrait.** "Aaron Bidwell did not read. He had a person for that." He owns the controlling
piece of the company Elijah works for and has never been in the building. "Have I been there?"
No. "Good. Go on."

He lifts "one finger" and the reader stops. He holds his empty glass out into the aisle "without
looking, and somebody took it, because somebody always did." On the steps of a dinner he puts an
empty glass in Elijah's hand "without looking at him." What he registers is "a guy in a bad
jacket, holding a notebook." "Faces rarely did" ring a bell. "He remembered what people owned."

"A map was the one thing he would look at with his own eyes." On it are "red parcels, fifty-three
of them, growing toward each other like a rash," and one hole. He puts "one finger on the hole,
lightly." When he is beaten he "thought about being angry. It was a lot of effort and it had
never once improved a map."

In the last room, "a cold room with no windows, on what had been a dairy," he puts one finger on
the steel, "lightly, the way he'd once touched a map." "His mouth had gone dry."

**Reads as.** He has never had to look at a person, and the first thing that ever looks back at
him is the one he bought.

**Voice.** "And nobody knew it was worth a dime until a machine showed up hungry." "Find out what
the dirt costs." "Nobody does anymore." "Nobody just wants that." "Find out where he lands. Then
buy what's next to it." "And I'm losing to a widow and a welder." "What do I own?" "Hush fucking
hush." "Does it know it's mine?"

**His plate.** The empty glass he holds out without looking.

**Blank.** His face, his age, his house, his family, where the money started.

**Suggestions.**
1. "Did not read." Will not, or cannot? Reuben's grandfather "lost sixty acres to a signature he
   couldn't read." Do not settle it. Let the reader hold both.
2. Somebody always takes the glass. In the cold room, once, he should hold something out and
   nobody takes it.
3. Keep him without a face. He does not see them, so we do not see him.
