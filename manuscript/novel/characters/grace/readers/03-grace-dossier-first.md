## (a) Contradictions / continuity items (both places quoted)

1.  Where she told him the fire-district story. 2193-2200 is told in the shouse by the stove after eleven, lamps off? The text does not say dark. 2954 she says "I told you that in the dark." Possibly figurative; the scene is lit by the stove. Low risk, but flag.
2.  "A knock." 2260 "That was the night she knocked." then 2261 "One knock. Then the latch" and her line "I didn't wait." Both can be read as one thing (knock then enter) but the narration and her line pull in opposite directions. Compare 1878 and 2609, where Marta's "knocked once and let herself in" is the same act and Grace calls it Marta's way (1905 "like Marta"). So at 2262 "I didn't wait" matches Marta; at 2247 she tells Elijah "you knock ... He gets to slam it in your face." No contradiction in the facts, but her own rule (let him slam it) and her practice (knock once, enter) differ and the text only comments at 2765.
