## L. Contradictions and tensions

Strict (both cannot be true):
1. Distance from the stove. 2710: "She'd watched him do it for a year and a half from as far across a room as she could get." Against 2225 (moves the chair "a foot closer"), 2272 (brings the chair over beside him, the same autumn), and 2346 (she lives and sleeps in his place by summer of the next year, with the Fisher in it). The narrator's 2911 ("couldn't sit within eight feet") fits 2196, but not 2272.
2. "Never once reached for hers." 2989: "he had never once reached for hers." Against 2720 ("He crossed the room"), 2775 ("they were holding hands"), 2721, where he reaches for her or holds her. Only true if "hers" means her arm and nothing else.
3. Whose hand at 1837. The hand under the table is not named as hers. Counted above with a caveat.

Tensions (could be reconciled):
4. "I waited all week" (2931) against 2894 to 2905: on Wednesday he says he is letting the arms go and she says "Okay." She still says she kept looking up the driveway for a truck.
5. Hitch voice. 1858 uses it on herself; 2077 says she "had never once pointed it at him." Not a contradiction, but the voice is not only an anger voice.
6. Pay. 1599 "I want to pay you." 1775 "I just have to bring it right." 2345 "a year and a half late." Elapsed time from arrival (October) to the summer vote is about nine months in year two or a year and nine months in year three. The text says "a year and a half." 2347 to 2349 says she told him an amount; the amount is never stated.
7. The fire district. 2199 says she knocked and told them it was time; 2205 has the captain say "Every time"; 2203 is a single night. Reads as one story with repeated days.
8. 3080: "Grace lit the Fisher at noon." 3011: Elijah told Devendra "Keep the stove going," and 3007 left it banked "so it would last." Not explained why she lights it.
9. Tape on the gatepost. 1760: "faded it almost to pink," "there a while." The text never says who tied it or whether Grace did; 3048 to 3050 treat the new strip as hers. 2621's door tape is unattributed.
10. Manuscript. 1810: "It was on the cover page of the manuscript, which was on the desk in the room Grace had found him." Priya knows the title. The text does not say who read it; 1785 mentions a cot, not a desk.
11. 2630 Marta: "Grace told me your bar story." The page shows Elijah tells it only to Grace (2208). No conflict, but the same night 2610 to 2616 Grace hears "You're the lamp" and Marta is not told it.
