## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

**Portrait.** "An old guy in a canvas jacket," "sixty-something, with a white beard cut short and
hands that had grease in the knuckles that was never coming out." He is walking down a row of
six robot arms, yanking on ratchet straps. "Gus. Grab that end. I don't like this one." He
checks every strap twice and then stands back and looks at the load "the way Elijah's mother
looked at a pie."

He drives "like a man with eggs in the back, five under the limit in the right lane." In the cab:
"sunflower seeds in the cup holder and a thermos that said IRON DERBY on the side, with most of
the letters worn off." The radio is "a ranchera station." He buys two coffees "and wouldn't take
the three dollars."

He describes a fortune "like a guy describing how he'd backed into a mailbox." He has a museum in
an old Elks lodge and four short buses. "One's got a kiln in the back. One's got a printing
press, an old iron one, weighs more than the bus."

Elijah turns him down. "He was the same guy he'd been a mile ago. He asked if Elijah wanted the
rest of the sunflower seeds." At the dock he checks the straps "one last time, even though the
trip was over," writes his number "on the back of a gas receipt," and mentions the book. Elijah
had said that once, "on the podcast, in the second hour, when he figured nobody was still
listening."

**Reads as.** He is the proof that a yes can come with no hook in it, and he listens to the
second hour.

**Voice.** "It's always a strap, man. It's never the two million dollars." "I can't watch that.
It's like watching somebody shoot a horse." "Nine seasons. It never once did anything
interesting. Folks loved it." "That's the nicest thing anybody's said to me since the divorce."
"I've owned one since 1994. It's called an endowment." "It's the only smart thing I ever did on
purpose." "The ones for nothing are the expensive ones." "Your choice. I can't force you to do
it." "Love that for you."

**His plate.** The thermos.

**Blank.** His surname. Where the shop is. The ex-wife. Children.

**Suggestions.**

2. His show was a robot with no weapon that "just pushed everybody into the wall, really
