Derby with his son every week and said Gus was "the only guy on TV who drove like him." When Gus

Manny carries the stove up a flight of stairs, down again and into the truck. He drops the well
line twice. He is "always right," according to David, and "it's the worst thing about him." He
never speaks. The uncle thinks it is the pump.
