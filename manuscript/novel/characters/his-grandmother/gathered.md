## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

**Portrait.** She put his hand flat on the madrones when he was small. "That's your tree. It can
burn to the ground and come back from the root. Remember that." She heated a farmhouse with one
stove. She "cooked on it, dried socks over it, kept lambs alive behind it in bad Februaries," on
wood "she'd bucked herself from her own downed oaks." When her shoulder went there was "the
borrowed trailer, the neighbor who helped." She "knew what it needed and who she could call."
She "never once worried about the valley she couldn't farm."

**Reads as.** The co-op, forty years early and one woman wide. Everything Elijah learns up north
she already did.

**Her plate.** The Fisher, "four hundred pounds of plate steel with a crack in one firebrick."

**Blank.** Her name, her face, when she died, whether there was a husband.

**Suggestion.** Nothing to add. She is finished. The one loose thread is that she has a bad
shoulder and so does Marta, and the book never lets Elijah notice.
