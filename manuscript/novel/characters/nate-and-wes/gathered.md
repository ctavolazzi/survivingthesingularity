## Character arcs, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-ARCS-2026-11-18.md`.

Seventeen years of load-ins. In Ch 2 Wes texts "is my stuff in that thing." The honest answer is
yes, and Elijah helped build the pipe that took it. He drafts three true sentences, sends "Let
me look into it," and "He never did." On Main Street they look away and drive off (Ch 14). They
are never seen again. Chapter 39 settles David, his mother, the three, Gus and Devendra. Not them.

There is a strong irony sitting unused here. Elijah spends five years grieving three strangers he
did not harm. The one person he did wrong, who asked him a straight question, never gets an
answer.

*Proposal:* in Ch 39, after David tells him the three were fine, he answers Wes. The three true
sentences he deleted in Ch 2, sent five years late. No reply comes, and none is needed. He
knocked. David can cover the band in a breath ("They got a keyboard player. They're booked.").
That is "You're not that powerful" again: the band went on without him too.
*For Nate:* he needs a reason of his own in Ch 14. The cheapest one is that the video cost the
band. They became the prophet's band, and bookings dried up.
*Risk:* chapter 39 already hands out three books. A fourth settling could turn it into a
checklist. If only one thing is added to that chapter, this is the one, because it is the only
debt that is real.

## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

*Added 19 November: "The guitar is gone for good." When Elijah asks his followers for help, Nate writes to tell him to take the band's name off the page. In chapter 39 Elijah finally answers Wes, and Wes sends a thumb.*

**Portrait.** Bass and drums. Seventeen years of loading "the same amps into the same van." Wes
has made every Second Crop flyer since 2006, on Claypot software, "and for the last four years
he'd paid for it." Gig posters, "a logo for his cousin's taqueria, the album art for a record
that sold ninety copies." About thirty of the band page's two hundred followers are related to
him. He sends one text in the book: "is my stuff in that thing." To the video, "Nate had sent a
thumb. Wes hadn't sent anything."

On Main Street Elijah knows "exactly how Nate would set his end down." Then "Wes looked away."
"Nate looked down, at the case, like it needed his attention."

**Reads as.** Wes is the one who was actually robbed, by a thing his friend helped build, and who
asked once and was not answered. Nate lent the paperback that named the whole business and wants
no part of what came of it.

**Their plate.** The keyboard case. "Second Crop had never had a keyboard."

**Blank.** Everything physical. Their ages, day jobs, surnames. Neither has a spoken line.

**Suggestions.**
1. "He knew exactly how Nate would set his end down." Tell us how. One clause, and Nate exists.
2. Wes signs things. A man who has drawn every flyer since 2006 has a mark in the corner. If
   Elijah once saw that mark, or something too close to it, come out of the machine, then "Yes
   and I built the part that" has a picture attached.
3. The van is the third member. "The same van" since high school. What is still in it that is
   Elijah's?
