## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

**Portrait.** "A small woman with a pencil behind her ear." She reads "with her pencil in her
hand, the way she read everything." The first thing she says to Elijah is about his list:
"There's a third one. It's in your title." He has told nobody the title. It is on the cover page
of a manuscript on a desk in his room.

She puts the trial beds on the worst ground on the place, a pad "packed to a hardpan that rang
under a shovel like a sidewalk." "Anyone can garden in good dirt. The book we're writing needs
the parking lot." She fences off the good-looking end: "The people eating this can't be the
experiment." When his dashboard says the beds are wet, she "looked at his printout the way Marta
had once looked at his renders. Then she handed him the shovel." When the numbers come out his
way, "Elijah wanted to call it proof. Priya wrote it down in pencil."

On his last morning she does not stop what she is doing. She reaches into her apron and gives him
"a paper sample bag and a pencil, a new one, sharpened." "Dig one at home. Tell me what you find
at thirty."

**Reads as.** She will not call anything proven. She writes in pencil because she expects to be
corrected, and she picked the worst dirt on purpose.

**Voice.** "Nobody asks Reuben. Reuben notices." "Then we took his confidence for a check." "Your
sensor lives at one centimeter. The root lives at thirty." "It's not a recipe." "Let's sit with
it." "Hello, ridge."

**Her plate.** The pencil.

**Blank.** Surname, age, who she lives with, where she worked, why she left.

**Suggestions.**
1. He writes in pen, "pressing hard enough to emboss the next three pages," so nobody can say he
   did not see it coming. She writes in pencil. That is the whole difference between them, and
   the book has not yet put the two in one sentence.
2. Her past is already one line, and it is a good one: "Every extension office I ever worked in
   had that framed on the wall, and every budget I ever sat through funded the crust anyway."
   I would not add to it.
3. She reads other people's cover pages and tells them. She "had noticed the lights" when Elijah
   was going under in chapter 18. She is the one who sees first, every time. Let it be her who
   notices Marta's cup.
