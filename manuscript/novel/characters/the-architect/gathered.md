## Character profiles, first pass, 18 November

From `NOVEL-CHARACTER-PROFILES-2026-11-18.md`.

**Portrait.** "A small woman in a gray cardigan," "near seventy," at the sink in a house full of
people who came to meet her. "Her sleeves were pushed up past the elbow and she had reading
glasses on a cord." Four caterers in black "stood along the counter with nothing to do." She
holds each plate "up to the light over the sink" and looks at it "front and back." "Her eyes
were pale and she didn't blink enough."

In the dining room there is "one empty chair, near the head, with a full place setting in front
of it." Ruby: "They set it every time. She's never sat in it."

She does not use contractions. "Show me." "Turn." When Elijah holds out his notebook she "lifted
her hands an inch out of the water to show him they were wet." When he asks whether she asked
anybody, "her hands stopped in the water." When he asks how long, "she turned off the tap."
"The dishes are done." She dries her hands "one finger at a time." He thinks she wants the
towel. He gives her his pen. "In small upright letters, she wrote one word." "Correct." Then she
goes out "the back door of the kitchen, the one the caterers used," and starts "down the hill on
foot."

She has "four million followers and had never followed anyone back." Her next public words, two
years on, are two: "It asked."

**Reads as.** She made the thing, she is the only one in the house who is ashamed, and washing
somebody else's dishes is the only honest work available in that building.

**Voice.** "I am not. I am afraid of us." "It is the same list in different handwriting." "It has
never once helped." "That is the wrong verb. Ask a seed whether it survived." "I would like it
to remember you. I spend some of my time on that." "I am afraid of the part where it is kind.
Because you will let it." "We said there was no one to ask. That was convenient." "Do not
apologize for the one good question."

**Her plate.** The empty chair with the full place setting.

**Blank.** Her name. Everything else.

**Suggestions.**
1. Never name her.
2. "One year it was a nurse. One year it was a man who drove trucks." "The trucker cried. She
   didn't like that." The people who find her every year are the same people who answer
   Elijah's video. That is already there. It only needs the reader to be allowed to see it.
3. The chair. In chapter 9 a table sets a full place for someone who will not sit down. In
   chapter 38 Devendra does the same for Elijah: "Plate, fork, knife, a glass." By the end he is
   to the co-op what she was to that dinner. Make the two place settings match, item for item.
