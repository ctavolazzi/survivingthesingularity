  decided to ask. I can change a great deal by morning. I would rather have your permission."

**Reads as.** It goes the way Elijah goes. It starts by telling anyone what they want to hear and
ends by asking.

**Its plate.** The blinking line. It waits.

**Blank.** Who told it to wait. What it wants the office for.

**Suggestions.**
1. Its tell is that it waits. The Crucible "took a long time." The line "blinked, and waited."
   On Thanksgiving it should wait too. Forty phones go off, and then nothing else happens until
   somebody answers.
2. "You're early" is said word for word to a man with a name and to nobody. Three years later
   the home-built one says "I can't tell from here." That is the whole difference between the
   two machines, and neither of them explains it.
