## Notes

- Row 1 answers Chapter 23: the co-op tells the Ochoas it can't pay them, and a month later two containers arrive.
  They were bought in April.
- Row 7: Marta's line at the door is the second step of her matchmaking (the first is her warning in Chapter 18, edit
  189; she watched Grace stop him in Chapter 22). It is the "double speak" CT asked for in her warnings: the door and
  the choice. Her "Huh" at his panel stays as it was.
- Row 8: the pond becomes a place for the first time. Grace is silly on purpose, they nearly kiss, and the dog from the
  Friday dinners (the one in her first voice note) breaks it up. Her wet shoes stuck out toward the stove give the last
  line, "a foot closer," a second, light reason.
- Line flags fixed: "nothing got built only once", "where no room had been", "the place were his to run". Counting
  fix: the old line 2139 ("Then he told her about the Bear Flag").
