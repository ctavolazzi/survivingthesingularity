At dinner one night Curtis set the laptop on the plywood ahead of the pie and turned it around.

"Laser weeder," he said. "Watch."

On the screen a steel box as wide as a two-car garage rode behind a tractor across a field of carrots. Under it the
weeds went up in little threads of smoke, one after another, and the carrots stood there untouched.

"We've all seen those," Tyler said. "The big growers run them. They're like a million dollars."

"That's last year's." Curtis tapped a key. "This one's got no computer doing the looking. Well, it's got a computer.

about the size of a lentil, and then they teach it. It eats sugar water. They call it a brain organoid."

**"You mean…" Priya said with horror, "that weeder is piloted by a real brain?"**

**"Piloted? Like a mech?" Curtis said, leaning back from the screen. "Nah, this is Optimus friggin Prime, baby! This is
bona fide synthetic life."**

**Ever-oblivious to the moods around him, Curtis forged on.**

**"This is the beginning of a new form of life. Half biology, half metal."**

**Priya physically shivered, noticeably.**

**"We are not having that on our farm," Marta said with definitive certainty. Putting her whole foot down, she simply

**"Absolutely fucking not."**

**"I'm out, Curtis," Wren said, wrapping her shawl tight around her shoulders.**

**"You knew this was coming," Grace said, standing up. "The rest of us can always count on you for the weird shit."**

**"Aye aye, captain." Curtis made an exaggerated salute.**

**Just about everyone got up and left, including Elijah, who followed Grace outside.**

It was fine, she said. She was gone before he could ask what festival, and before anybody had cut the pie.
