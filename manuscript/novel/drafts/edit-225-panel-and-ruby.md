## Ch 8, the panel, after the woman defines AGI

### Book now

The room was kind about it. People patted him afterward. So real, somebody said. It was worse than if they'd laughed.

### Change to

The man on Elijah's right was a sneakers one. He had a big laugh, and he'd been using it on the front row since they sat down.

**"Okay. AGI,"** he said, turning in his chair. **"So if that happens, what's to stop it from just destroying everyone and everything?"**

**"A lot of people have been working on that,"** Elijah said. **"It's something called the alignment problem."**

**"Alignment problem? You mean like my car?"**

**Everyone laughed.**

**"So why don't we just take the AI to the mechanic?"**

**"Well, there isn't really a mechanic for what this thing could do."**

The laughing stopped.

**"You mean like something out of a sci-fi movie? I thought all that stuff was a few hundred years away."**

**People chuckled, but nervously.**

**"Most of the leading experts, at least from what I've read, say that AGI could arrive within the next ten years."**

**"So what's your prediction?"**

Devendra had said the surveys put it at thirty years, maybe forty. Elijah could say thirty. The whole room would breathe easier, and half of them would like him better for it. But the date was in the video, in his own voice.

**"I said it in the video,"** he said. **"And I wasn't just trying to be, you know, sensational."**

**"So you really believe we're going to get a machine that can replace all humans? By 2027."**

The room went so quiet he could hear people shifting in their chairs. Somebody coughed.

There was still time to lie. Elijah looked out past the lights and decided not to.

**"I mean, yeah,"** he said. **"That's what the math says."**

A groan went through the seats, and then everybody was murmuring at once.

**"Well, you heard it here first, folks,"** the man said, to the room. **"It's all over. You can pack it up. The killer robots are coming before the end of the decade."**

**"Hang on. I never said anything about killer robots."**

**"Get to your bunker. Kiss your dog and your family goodbye. Elijah Madrone here says it's the end of the world before the end of the decade."**

That got the big laugh back. The moderator thanked everybody in his very calm voice, and it was over.

People patted him afterward. *So real,* somebody said. Nobody asked him about the math.
