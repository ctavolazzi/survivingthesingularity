## 1. The end of Chapter 16 (added after "Then I'll show you where you sleep.")

Half a dozen people had come down to unload the trailer. They were passing pans out of it hand to hand, and they were
in the middle of an argument.

**"The artists have a valid point, don't they?"** a woman said.

**"What do you mean?"**

**"Well, they are losing income to a machine. They are being replaced in the corporate hierarchy. They're losing quite
a bit of their power."**

**"Yeah, but they're kind of doing it to themselves, aren't they?"** a man said from inside the trailer. **"With all
their witch hunts and their constant complaining in every comment section, they're not exactly doing themselves a favor
with all that."**

**"They will adapt, as the lamplighters did."**

That came from behind Elijah. A man had turned the corner of the shed with a bucket in each hand.

"Lou," Grace said. "This is him."

**He was imposing, a big guy,** about sixty, sunburned right up to his hat line. **But there was a certain temperance
to him that was noticeable. He moved gently. And while his boots were quite large to accommodate such big feet, he had
a light step, instead of pounding the ground like so many men his size. Lou's steps were light as a feather.**

**Elijah was reminded of his uncle, who was a Hopi tribesman. They weren't related by blood, but that uncle had
imparted a lot of lessons when Elijah was growing up, and was a big reason why Elijah eventually became an Eagle Scout.
But that's another story for another time.**

Lou set the buckets down.

**"Everyone, please welcome our new guest, Elijah,"** he said. **"He'll be staying with us for a while."**

A few of them lifted a hand. Somebody said, "Hey, Elijah." The pans started moving again.

Notes: his dictation put into the past tense; "well" kept only where it is the speaker's. "about sixty, sunburned right
up to his hat line" is the book's own description of Lou, moved here from the Friday dinner. Chapter 17 still opens on
his line, "For three days, no one really asked him anything."
