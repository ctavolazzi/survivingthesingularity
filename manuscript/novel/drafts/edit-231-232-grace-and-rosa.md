## Where each part went, and why

His dictation splits in three along its own seams:

1. **"Nobody asked him any questions for about 3 days"** and **"Mijo, you look muy flaco"** are already in the book.
   Chapter 17 opens on "For three days, no one really asked him anything.", with "He looks thin." through the wall of
   the kitchen trailer, and Rosa says "Mijo, you look muy flaco. Come in, eat." at her door (his edit 110). Nothing
   added.
2. **The ladle** is "later in the book", as he said. It opens Chapter 31, the Sunday after the county meeting where
   Rosa spoke with a minute left and Elijah's slides fell apart. Her "Sometimes you can over-prepare" pays off two lines
   later in the book as it stands: "No laser pointer. One page. He had learned that much." and "He'd kept it under three
   minutes." Patricia's "Rosa doesn't get a year." comes a page after. The tamales come back in Chapter 38.
3. **The talk about the bed** has to be in his first days ("when do I start paying", "stay here for free"), so it goes
   in Chapter 17. Rosa cannot send him to Grace then: she lives in a converted garage in town, her wrist is in a brace,
   and he first meets her on Curtis's route weeks later. So the talk starts from Grace's own habit on the page ("Grace
   parked a plate in front of him ... and stood there until it was empty"): one night she sits down with her own
   plate. Rosa's "Please take this to Grace. It's her turn to eat." stays with Rosa, at the end of her scene.

The pay: **"You can stay here for free as long as you like"** is the room. Her message already said "Your arms can live
there for free while you figure it out. That's not a trick. We just have the room." and "You'd be a guy with a room."
**"So when do I start paying"** is him offering rent. The pay she promised him ("I want to pay you", Ch 15; "About the
pay." / "It has to go to the table", Ch 16) still goes to the table and is voted at half in Chapter 26.

## 1. Edit 231, Chapter 17 (after "she let the six lines be all of it.", before "Marta gave him the floor.")

One night, when his plate was empty, she sat down across from him with her own.

**"How are you liking your bed? Is it comfy enough?"**

**He raised an eyebrow. "Yeah, it's great. It gets a little cold in there at night, but you guys give me plenty of
blankets, so I feel really good. Thank you. I'm really grateful for this."**

**"No problem."**

**"So when do I start paying, or sign, or whatever?"**

**"No, I meant it. You can stay here for free as long as you like. It's just good to have people like you around, you
know?"**

**"What do you mean, people like me?"**

**"You know, people who get it. Every one of these families came to us in one way or another, but we're all
contributing to the success of the farm." She started eating. "I trust you'll do the same."**

**He thought about it,** and **lingered a minute. "Is this too good to be true?"**

**"What? You want something?"**

**He raised his eyebrows and blinked a few times. "No, I'm good."**

**"Great. Close the door on your way out, would you?"**

Taken out: the fillers "like" and "he's like", and "pauses for a minute" folded into "lingered a minute". "Is this too
good to be true?" is said out loud, as he dictated it ("He's like, is this too good to be true?"). Claude's: the first
sentence, and "and" in "He thought about it, and lingered a minute."

## 2. Edit 232, Chapter 31 (a new first scene, then a scene break, before "Elijah brought the Friday table a plan.")

The Sunday after the county meeting, Elijah carried pots at the grange hall. Rosa was serving. She had seen his slides.

**"I don't really understand all this technology stuff,"** she said. **"It goes over my head. In one ear, out the
other."**

**She paused, turned around with the ladle,** and filled a bowl. **"I do know good food. In this place, they make a lot
of good food. Nourishes the whole community." She pointed at him and tapped him on the chest. Then she put her whole
palm on his chest and rubbed it in circles. "You have the heart of a helper. I can tell."**

**"I just want everybody to be prepared,"** Elijah said.

**"Prepared is sometimes not helpful. Sometimes you can over-prepare. If I over-prepare my tamales, they fall apart.
They don't taste too good."**

**Elijah kind of got it.**

She held out the bowl. **"Please take this to Grace. It's her turn to eat."**

Slips fixed: "I do no good food" is "I do know good food"; "one in one ear" is "In one ear". He restated the chest
beat ("taps him on the chest ... rubs his chest, like taps him on the chest, and then puts her whole palm on his
chest"); the last version is the line. Claude's: the three opening sentences, "filled a bowl" for his "drops it in the
bowl" (so it can't read as dropping the ladle), and "She held out the bowl" (the same bowl goes to Grace). "Rosa was
serving", not cooking: in February her wrist is still in the brace (Ch 30), and it comes out only in Chapter 38.
