# Edit 222: the last continuity pass over the whole book (26 Nov, ninth chat)

CT, 26 Nov: "what I keep wanting you to check for are continuity errors, confusing moments, repetition, bad sentence
structure, anything that might cause problems for the reader ... Anything that doesn't make any sense." Then: "Go on the pass."

How it was done: the book as built after edit 221 (`make_v6.py`, 103,042 words) was frozen and split into eight ranges.
Eight readers each read one range line by line and checked its facts against the rest of the book, under one brief
(continuity, confusing, sense, repetition, structure; not CT's taste). They returned 79 findings. A script checked
every quote and every proposed find against the text (79 of 79 exact). Claude then read each one on the page and
judged it: 50 fixed here (51 ops rows; E2 needed two), 10 held for CT (listed at the end), 19 dropped (not an error on reading, a deliberate
echo, or CT's own words where the change would be taste). Line numbers are in the frozen text.

All wording below is Claude's unless marked. Where a fix touches a line CT dictated, it says so.
