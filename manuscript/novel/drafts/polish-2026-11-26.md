# Edit 227: the polish pass at key moments (26 November 2026, tenth logging chat)

CT: "Any sentences that are left over from all of our passes that still don't quite hold up to the standard that we're
setting ... Not every stanza, not every paragraph needs to be a work of fucking art, but there are some key moments
where there's still opportunity to buff it out and polish it up. I'd like you to do a pass to tighten everything here
and now."

How it was done: four readers each read one range of the book whole (Chapters 0 to 15; 16 to 26 and "Pondwater"; 27 to
33; 34 to the Epilogue) with `WRITING-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, and returned 50 sentences at key moments. Every quote was
machine-matched to the text (all 50 matched). Claude read each one on the page and checked whose words it was (the
bold in `drafts/`, and `EDITS.md`). 21 ops rows fix 20 places; 30 were left. Nothing of CT's dictated wording was
changed. Every replacement is Claude's wording. No fact was invented. 58 words in, 78 out.
