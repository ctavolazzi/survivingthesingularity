# Surviving the Singularity: the habits pass, before and after

Made 28 Nov 2026 for CT, to read beside "StS v8 large print CURRENT (28 Nov, edits 1 to 322, the habits pass).pdf". Page numbers are that PDF's.

What this pass did: it looked at eight repeated habits from Chapter 11 to the end ("the way ...", "looked at him", "Then he / Then she", "for a while / a long time", "He knew / didn't know", "nodded / shrugged", "didn't say anything", "It wasn't X. It was Y"). Only places where one habit piled up in one scene were touched, and one instance stayed in each. 159 lines were in pile-ups: 49 changed (48 changes here, plus the workbench line at the end), 105 were kept as the strongest or as plain and right, and 7 rewrites were tried and turned down, so those lines stayed. Your dictated lines were left alone. Every change is Claude's wording unless it says yours.

To undo any one, say which. It comes out as its own edit.
