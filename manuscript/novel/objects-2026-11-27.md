- A sighting is a line that puts the thing somewhere. Mentions in passing ("the Telecaster he'd pawned") are in only

## What holds

Nothing is in two places at once. The amp goes closet, living room, Ma's porch, Ma's barn, the cab (belted, five
months), the co-op truck, the shouse door, the pond, the stove, and stays. The pot leaves Ma's stove burned, rides in the
Ranger, turns up in the co-op kitchen trailer (Grace carries it back in, Ch 22), leaves in the Ranger and ends in Manny's
rack. The notebooks go from one to two (Ch 5) to eleven (Ch 35, 37, 39). The pawn ticket sits behind Gus's gas receipt
in the same notebook in Ch 15 and Ch 17; the Telecaster is in the window by May and gone by Ch 39. Curtis's hose goes on
the dash, then the glove box, on top of the closing papers, and is still there the day he leaves. The arms go dock,
storage unit, Praether's barn, and are left there; his lie that they went at a lien sale is the only other place they
are ever said to be.

## Findings (only two kinds: the page contradicts itself, or something is set up and never paid)

1. **The pouch's set list.** Ch 5: the pouch holds "picks, a capo, a spare cable, a set list folded in quarters." Ch 37:
   he folds his note in quarters, "like a set list," and puts it "in the leather pouch on the side of the amp, under the
   picks." Ch 38: Grace finds "Picks. A capo. A set list folded in quarters, except it wasn't a set list." Either the old
   set list has vanished or she finds only one of two folded pages. Small, and a reader who remembers Ch 5 may stop on
   it. Smallest fix, Claude's wording, in Ch 37: "...put it in the leather pouch on the side of the amp, under the picks."
   becomes "...put it in the leather pouch on the side of the amp, in place of the old set list." (The spare cable is
   gone by Ch 38 too, which nobody will notice.)

Nothing set up with an object goes unpaid: the tape, the hose, the receipt, the pawn ticket, the checks and the note
all come back.
