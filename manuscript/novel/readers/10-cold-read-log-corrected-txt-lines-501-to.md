## 7. WHAT HE WANTS, ACT BY ACT

- ACT ONE (519-847): Elijah wants someone to tell him he is overreacting, then, failing that, wants proof the machine has a ceiling so he can sleep. What stops him is that nobody around him will look at it, that the ceiling is only a power bill, and that his own complicity (the pipeline, the napkin) won't leave him.
- ACT TWO (848-1726): Elijah wants to be heard and believed, and then, after the stroke, simply to keep his mother safe and not to be a burden to anyone. What stops him is his own habit of saying yes to anyone who flatters him, the cruelty and contempt of the crowd, the loss of money, friends and band, and his mother's stroke.
- ACT THREE (1727-2632): Elijah wants to belong at the co-op and to turn what he knows into something useful and big. What stops him is Marta and the others insisting on small, slow, human-scale, free work, and his own repeated errors (the leak, node 7) and impulse to pitch, scale and rescue.
- ACT FOUR (2633-3269): Elijah wants to warn people and to make the co-op act (with the arms, then the truth at the fire), and to keep Grace. What stops him is the co-op's refusal, Grace's demand that they save everybody, Lou's "you can't change anyone's mind," and his own slow acceptance of what he does and doesn't control.
