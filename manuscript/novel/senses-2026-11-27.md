# The senses pass: notes

Started 27 Nov 2026, sixteenth chat, at CT's word ("Can we make a senses spreadsheet in a way that helps cover the text
and look for opportunities to add or subtract, modify or whatever the physical senses of the scene? In a minimal and
professional manner"). The sheet is `senses-2026-11-27.csv` (data: `senses-data-2026-11-27.json`), the "Senses" tab of
the story checks workbook, rebuilt by `story_checks.py senses`. It is an inventory: every sound, touch, smell and taste
line on the page, the strongest things seen, food eaten with no taste given, whose words each smell and taste line is,
and a column for CT's call. No scores.

## How the pass runs (CT agreed the plan, 27 Nov)

1. Close what the smell pass left open.
2. The sheet, as an inventory.
3. Claude reads every flagged scene and every scene that already has a smell or taste, in place, and decides leave,
   cut, sharpen or add. Most land on leave.
4. CT gets only what survives, each argued from the page.
5. CT's lines first; anything Claude words is shown before it is logged.

Never add a sense because a scene lacks one. Sound is already working.
