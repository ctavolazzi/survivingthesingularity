# Publication design plan

Manuscript baseline: v0.8.2, 30 sections. Publication design is a separate rendering of the unchanged manuscript. All previous editions remain available.

1. **Audit**: inventory included art, licensing, typography, captions, tables, references and current page flow. Preserve source hashes. Status: complete.
2. **Design system**: 6 x 9 inch trade interior, mirrored margins, a readable literary serif, restrained sans-serif labels, running heads, folios, chapter openings and part dividers. Original botanical and mechanical line art links nourishment with technology. Status: complete.
3. **Graphics**: create reproducible SVG illustrations and selective explanatory diagrams. Verify inherited images and embed licensed fonts. Maintain complete asset and credits records. Status: complete.
4. **Typesetting**: build a reading PDF with cover, a separate print interior, and editable HTML/CSS/SVG sources. Preserve narrative, references and existing captions; isolate any necessary rights substitutions in a manifest. Status: complete.
5. **Critique and revision**: inspect every page in contact sheets and representative pages at reading size, review independently, fix weak spacing and artwork, and verify text preservation, page bounds, links and fonts. Use deliberate failure controls for proof checks. Status: complete.
6. **Delivery**: save finished PDFs on Desktop, open reading edition in Preview, supply design report and production notes. Printer-specific cover wrap, ISBN and vendor approval remain tied to the selected publishing route. Status: complete.

## Design direction

Calm, tactile, hopeful. A serious argument about human survival should read like a carefully made book. Off-white and green on the reading cover; black text on white interior pages; small olive accents in reading edition; black-only print edition. Images must clarify an idea or create a deliberate pause. No decorative stock-photo accumulation. Small original graphics are authored as editable SVG, with no external art dependency.

## Preservation

Baseline copies and SHA-256 values are in this directory. New builds are written under publication/output. Manuscript source, older PDFs and original art remain intact. Rights replacements occur only in the publication transformation and are recorded explicitly.
