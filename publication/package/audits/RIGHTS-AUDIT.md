### Source quality and attribution corrections

- **Own-work declarations:** ASIMO, madrone bark, chessboard, Spot, solar barn, Wikimedia servers, workbench, and Galloway carry creator licensing declarations. Mattes's image carries an owner public-domain dedication. Galloway offers multiple alternatives; this edition selects CC BY 2.5 and preserves Stefan-Xp's compass-rose credit. Its source also identifies NASA SRTM topography and an 1821 boundary map. This audit did not reconstruct a separate chain of title for those older inputs.
- **Flickr imports:** the factory exhibit and 3D printer have Commons Flickr-review records confirming BY-SA 2.0 and BY 2.0 at import. Direct author-side pages were attempted but did not return readable text in the web tool. Sophia's ITU Flickr page and Commons review record were retrieved. SpaceX's file carries CC0 and a Commons permission-ticket reference; the private correspondence was not accessible. These are documented source chains, not fresh permission letters.
- **M87:** [ESO's image record](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1907a/) credits EHT Collaboration. [ESO's policy](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/) permits credited CC BY 4.0 reuse. Preserve the inherited crop and TIFF-to-JPEG conversion notice.
- **Hubble:** Commons identifies a pre-2009 public-domain basis. The current [ESA/Hubble image record](https://esahubble.org/images/heic0406a/) and [usage policy](https://esahubble.org/copyright/) also supply explicit CC BY 4.0 distribution terms, selected for this edition. Give the full NASA, ESA, S. Beckwith (STScI), HUDF Team credit and the inherited black-point edit by Noodle snacks. The current HubbleSite copyright endpoint timed out and is not claimed as newly inspected.
- **NASA and DARPA:** file-specific government-work descriptions were checked against [NASA's image guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) and [DARPA's usage policy](https://www.darpa.mil/policies/usage-policy). These do not imply endorsement or license trademarks. The historical Atlas image endpoint was not independently recovered; the exact Commons file and documented agency basis were inspected.
- **Solar barn:** the creator explicitly specifies **Reinhold Möller**, not merely the account name Ermell.
- **Caption identity:** the Jaguar robot photograph shows an interactive museum exhibit, not a working factory production line. The Haifa photograph shows an abandoned bar inside an old shopping mall, not proof the entire mall was abandoned. Corrections are recorded for the publication captions.
- **Format:** the Galloway local file is PNG despite its `.jpg` extension. Do not claim a JPEG conversion occurred.

CC BY and BY-SA permit commercial reuse under their conditions. Preserve title and attribution, exact license links and alteration notices. Grayscale adaptations of ShareAlike images stay under their named image license. That does not automatically relicense the independent text of the book. See [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), [BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), [BY 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), and [Creative Commons' collection guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#if-i-create-a-collection-that-includes-a-work-offered-under-a-cc-license-which-licenses-may-i-choose-for-the-collection).

## Copyright-page requirements and limits

Identify author, year, and edition accurately. Exclude third-party and public-domain materials from a blanket ownership claim; preserve their licenses and credit AI-assisted illustration production accurately. Do not invent a publisher, imprint, ISBN, registration, or permission grant, or state all permissions have been obtained. An “all rights reserved” notice should expressly preserve the separately licensed material's terms.

This is a source-and-license audit, not a global legal opinion, model-training-data audit, or trademark/personality clearance. No external rights requests were sent. The material unresolved external-publication decision is treatment of modern or still-protected epigraphs, particularly Vinge, Good, and Mitchell. A private design proof can preserve these while the external-release decision remains explicit.
