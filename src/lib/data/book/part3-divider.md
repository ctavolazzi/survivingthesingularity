# Part III: How to Survive the Transition

![Pixel art: a container shouse with a lit window and rooftop solar, a wind turbine, garden rows, and a LoRa mast under a starry sky](/book-images/part3-divider.png)

Begin with food reaching a person. Use existing relationships and tools where they work; compare new methods on a bounded task. You can take part without owning land, a rack of servers, or a robot. Wider possibilities remain available as the work develops.
