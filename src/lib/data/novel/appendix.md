# Appendix: The People, the Places and the Sides

## A note on the map

The co-op sits "up past Cloverdale," about three hours north of Santa Rosa. The real Cloverdale is under an hour up the 101. The distance was moved for fiction purposes.

## The four sides

By Thanksgiving 2027 the co-op had split four ways at one table. Nobody in the book uses these names. They are here for readers who want them.

<strong>The Naturalists.</strong> Against the machines, all the way, and unlikely ever to come around. Other people call them Luddites, and Marta, who knows what the real Luddites were fighting, objects to the word. They are right about what is being lost. At the table: Wren, Patricia and the Sunday circle, with Curtis on his feet among them. Rosa belongs here too: "I've had enough changing."

<strong>The Accelerationists.</strong> Full steam ahead. Unafraid of the technology, early to every tool, ready to merge with it. At the table: Tyler and the two newest households, already spending it.

<strong>The We'll-Sees.</strong> One foot in and one foot out, named for Marta's answer to almost everything. Patient, not reckless. They wait for the last shoe to drop before they decide. At the table: Marta, Priya and Reuben, arms folded, in the middle where they always sat.

<strong>The Salt of the Earth.</strong> Regular people who want to get home to their families and get along with their friends, with no strong feelings either way. At the table: Denny, Frank, Irene and Lou. They kept the food moving.

Grace sat outside all four. She wanted the machine to stop asking and just do it.

## The people

<strong>Elijah Galen Madrone.</strong> Farm kid, musician, self-taught data guy. "Mad" to David, "college" to Marta and Lou.

<strong>Ma.</strong> Elijah's mother. Her stroke comes in the fall of 2024. "Barry?"

<strong>David McGrath.</strong> Bartender at the Bear Flag and Elijah's oldest friend. Ray McGrath, his father, hung the serenity prayer behind the bar.

<strong>Devendra.</strong> Elijah's colleague at Claypot. He made the bet: Thanksgiving 2027, loser cooks.

<strong>Nate and Wes.</strong> Second Crop, the band.

<strong>Manny.</strong> Ma's neighbor. He fixes things.

<strong>Melody.</strong> Elijah's half sister in Miami.

<strong>Aaron Bidwell.</strong> He holds the controlling piece of Claypot, eleven companies and fifty-three parcels. His reader has worked for him for ten years.

<strong>Ruby Roth-Jones.</strong> Founder of Porchlight, the lamp company.

<strong>The Architect.</strong> A woman near seventy who builds the machine that asks.

<strong>Gus.</strong> Iron Derby driver, art buses, the Elks lodge museum. Trent runs the Stockton cannery.

<strong>At the co-op.</strong> Grace runs the town kitchen and the route. Marta Okonkwo runs the shed. Denny Osei runs the channel. Lou grew pears outside Ukiah and grows most of what is on the table. Curtis drives the route. Priya digs the pits. Reuben Vance handles the part of the machine that runs on paper. Frank Kowalski ran the hardware store and then the tool library. Tyler writes the firmware. Patricia speaks for the grower network. Irene Calder owns the forty acres. Rosa packs fruit. Wren leads the Sunday circle.

## The places

<strong>The Bear Flag.</strong> David's bar, in the town where Elijah and David grew up.

<strong>The farm.</strong> Ma's place, ten miles out of town, with the madrones on the hill.

<strong>Claypot.</strong> The design software company where Elijah worked.

<strong>San Francisco.</strong> The panel, the Architect's kitchen, the fire escape.

<strong>Stockton.</strong> The cannery, two hours south of home.

<strong>Santa Rosa.</strong> Five weeks under a sycamore, and the old man on the curb.

<strong>The co-op.</strong> Nineteen households up past Cloverdale: the shed, the shouse, the seed house, the packing shed and Irene's forty acres.

<strong>The rose garden.</strong> Where Ma lives after the farm is sold.

## The timeline

<strong>Late 2022.</strong> Chat Horizon goes public. The toast at the Bear Flag. The bet.

<strong>2023.</strong> The stage list. The Fisher moves into the apartment.

<strong>Fall 2023.</strong> Elijah is laid off, eleven months after the toast.

<strong>Early 2024.</strong> San Francisco, the Architect and Ruby.

<strong>Summer 2024.</strong> Three weeks at the cannery.

<strong>September 2024.</strong> Ma's stroke. The farm is sold.

<strong>November 2024.</strong> North to the co-op. The broom.

<strong>2025.</strong> The controllers, Denny's channel, the Crucible and the leak. Ray dies. Irene's land goes into the trust. The first fire.

<strong>June 12, 2026.</strong> The big models go dark for eighteen days.

<strong>November 2026.</strong> The co-op ships its files free.

<strong>Early 2027.</strong> The annual meeting. Devendra arrives. The county funds the route through December.

<strong>March 2027.</strong> The machine on the dairy. Five small things go wrong.

<strong>July 9, 2027.</strong> The Architect posts "It asked."

<strong>Fall 2027.</strong> The robot arms, the festival, the bonfire. Elijah goes home.

<strong>Thursday, November 25, 2027.</strong> Thanksgiving.
